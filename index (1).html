03 / LIVE DEMO 01

One topic becomes
a five-part story.

Example: Cybersecurity awareness for students · 5-minute classroom presentation

01 / 0:39The HookWould you click?

A college email promises free notes or an internship. Would you trust the link?

02 / 0:57The ProblemWhy it matters

Show how phishing messages can trick students into sharing account details.

03 / 1:06The IdeaHow phishing works

Explain the trick in plain language, using one familiar example.

04 / 1:27The ActionPause. Check. Protect.

Verify the sender, inspect the link, use unique passwords and enable 2-step verification.

05 / 0:51The TakeawayMake it a habit

Leave one memorable message: stop, check, then click.
